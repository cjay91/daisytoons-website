# Questions about gifts

## Can you gift wrap my order?

Yes. Tick gift wrap at checkout. It costs Rs {gift_wrap_fee} for the whole order and comes with a handwritten card.

## Can I add a gift message?

Yes. Write your message at checkout, up to 300 characters, and we'll write it on the card by hand. It's free.

## Will the price be in the parcel?

No. Gift orders go out without prices in the parcel, so the person receiving it won't see what you paid.

## Can I send a gift straight to someone else?

Yes. Enter their name, address and phone number as the delivery address at checkout, and your own email so we can send you the confirmation and tracking.

## Can I choose the delivery day?

Not exactly. We send orders within one working day of payment, and delivery then usually takes 1–2 working days in Colombo and 2–5 elsewhere. For a birthday or other date, order about a week ahead to be safe.

## Do you do personalised or engraved gifts?

Some products are engraved or personalised; it says so in the product description. Personalised items can't be returned if you change your mind.
