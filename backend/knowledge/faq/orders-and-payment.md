# Questions about orders and payment

## How can I pay?

You pay online at checkout through PayHere, with a debit or credit card or any other method PayHere offers on the payment page. We never see your card details. We don't offer cash on delivery.

## My payment failed. What now?

Nothing is charged if a payment fails. Your items stay held for {reservation_minutes} minutes from checkout, so you can try again from your order page. After that, the hold ends and you'll need to check out again.

## How do I know my order went through?

We email you a confirmation with your order number as soon as your payment goes through. If it hasn't arrived within a few minutes, check your spam folder, then email us at {shop_email}.

## Can I change my order or the delivery address?

Email us at {shop_email} with your order number as soon as possible. We can change the address or items until the order has been sent. After that, it's up to the courier.

## Do I need an account to order?

No. You can check out as a guest. An account saves your addresses and lets you see all your orders in one place.

## How do I contact you?

Email {shop_email}. We reply within one working day.
