# Gift ideas for Sri Lankan occasions

## Avurudu

For Sinhala and Tamil New Year in April, greeting cards and tea hampers for the whole family are popular. Order by early April, as couriers are busy over the holidays.

## Vesak

For Vesak, simple and thoughtful gifts such as cards and decorative items suit the occasion. Avoid anything flashy.

## Christmas

Hampers, mugs and soft toys are our most popular Christmas gifts. Order by mid-December, since delivery takes longer in the last week before Christmas.

## Weddings and anniversaries

Couple photo frames and gifts tagged "For couples" are popular for weddings and anniversaries. Add a gift message with the couple's names.

## New baby

Soft toys and teddy bears are lovely for a new baby. Choose items without small parts.

## Thank-you gifts for teachers and colleagues

Engraved pens, tea hampers and mugs make good thank-you gifts. Look for items tagged "For teachers" or "For colleagues".

## Valentine's Day

Teddy bears, couple photo frames and cards are favourites for Valentine's Day. Order a week ahead, as February 14 is one of our busiest days.
