# Birthday gift ideas

## Birthday gifts for kids

Soft toys and teddy bears are the safest choice for young children. Look for gifts tagged "For kids". Add a gift message and gift wrap so it arrives ready to give.

## Birthday gifts for her

Mugs, photo frames and hampers are popular birthday gifts for mothers, sisters and friends. A hand-painted mug with a short message works well for someone you see every day.

## Birthday gifts for him

Tea hampers, engraved pens and photo frames suit fathers, brothers and colleagues. If you're not sure of their taste, a Ceylon tea hamper is hard to get wrong.

## Last-minute birthdays

If the birthday is in the next few days, choose items that are in stock and order early in the day. Delivery in Colombo usually takes 1–2 working days after we send the order.
