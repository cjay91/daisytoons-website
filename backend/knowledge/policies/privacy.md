# Privacy

<!-- Storefront page: /privacy -->
This explains what personal information Daisytoons collects when you shop with us, what we use it for, and your rights under Sri Lanka's Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022.

## What we collect

- Your name, email address and phone number, and the delivery address and gift message you give us.
- What you ordered and when, and the status of each order.
- If you create an account: your login details (your password is stored scrambled, never as text) and saved addresses.
- Basic technical information, such as your IP address, to keep the site secure and stop abuse.

We never see or store your card details. Payments are handled by PayHere, which tells us only whether a payment went through and its reference number.

## What we use it for

- To take, pack and deliver your order, and to email you about it.
- To answer your questions and handle returns.
- To keep records the law asks us to keep, such as sales records.

We don't sell your information, and we only send marketing emails if you've asked us to.

## Who we share it with

- The courier delivering your order: the recipient's name, address and phone number.
- PayHere, to take your payment.
- The companies that host our website and send our emails, only so they can do that for us.

## Cookies

We use only the cookies the shop needs to work: one to keep your cart, and one to keep you logged in. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

## How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as tax law requires (currently up to 7 years). If you delete your account, we remove your saved addresses and login, and keep only what we need for past orders.

## Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it where we don't need to keep it. Email {shop_email} and we'll reply within 21 days.
