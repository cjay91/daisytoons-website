# Returns and refunds

<!-- Storefront page: /returns -->

## Damaged, faulty or wrong items

If something arrives damaged, faulty or isn't what you ordered, email us within 7 days of delivery with your order number and a photo. We'll send a replacement or give you a full refund, including delivery, and arrange to collect the item if needed.

## Changing your mind

You can return unused items in their original packaging within 7 days of delivery. Email us first so we can tell you where to send them. Once they reach us, we refund the price of the items. Delivery fees and gift wrap aren't refunded, and the cost of sending the items back is yours.

We can't take back personalised items, food, flowers or anything that has been used.

## Cancelling an order

Email us as soon as possible. If your order hasn't been sent yet, we'll cancel it and refund you in full. Once it has been sent, it's treated as a return.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the card or account you paid with, through our payment provider PayHere. We'll email you when we make the refund. Your bank usually shows it within 5 to 10 working days.

This doesn't affect your rights under Sri Lankan consumer law.
