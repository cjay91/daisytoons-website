## How long it takes

We pack and send orders within one working day of payment. After that, delivery usually takes 1–2 working days in Colombo and 2–5 working days elsewhere. Around holidays such as Avurudu and Christmas it can take a little longer, so order early.

## Tracking

When your order leaves us, we email you the courier's name and tracking number. You can also see them on your order page, from the link in your confirmation email or in your account.

## Sending a gift

Gift orders go out without prices in the parcel. Add your message at checkout and we'll write it on the card. Please give a phone number the courier can reach, as they may call before delivering.

## If no one is home

The courier will usually call and try again. If a parcel comes back to us because the address was wrong or no one could be reached, we'll contact you to arrange another delivery, which may cost another delivery fee.
